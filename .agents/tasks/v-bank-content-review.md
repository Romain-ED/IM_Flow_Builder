The V Bank test fixture itself uses exactly this path (`LiveRcsControls.test.tsx:64-66`), while its assertion only searches for `demo|fictional|test`, so it passes despite the visible `Fake transport` text. This is an explicit artificial implementation label and development wording in a reachable V Bank journey/status surface, contrary to the requirement to remove demo/fictional/test/staging or obviously artificial copy. Replace or hide transport/storage internals for the V Bank branch (for example, neutral service/session wording), cover both fake and provider diagnostics, and assert the full rendered text excludes `fake`, `development`, `demo`, `fictional`, `example`, `staging`, `test`, and equivalent markers. Remove these unrelated changes from the reviewed change (or submit them as a separately approved task) before approval; do not accept a mixed diff as the V Bank content change.
